# Intake Bank (ask at most 4 - pick the ones that change the build)

**Revenue model** - one-off transaction / recurring membership / high-ticket consult-then-close /
retainer / e-commerce. Drives: Payments module, pipeline shape, whether subscriptions exist.

**Booking model** - solo provider / multi-provider round robin / team-panel collective /
class or group / multi-location / quote-first (no self-book). Drives: calendar type, distribution.

**Team & lead ownership** - who answers a new lead in business hours, and after hours?
Drives: round robin priority, notification actions, Voice AI after-hours agent, assignment logic.

**Average ticket & sales cycle length** - sets stage count, follow-up cadence length, and
whether a nurture track is worth building at all.

**Lead sources live today** - Google Ads / Meta Ads / GBP / organic / referral / walk-in.
Drives: source tags, attribution reporting, which triggers you actually need.

**Existing stack** - EHR/PMS/POS/ERP, accounting, payment processor already in use.
Drives: native vs LeadConnector API vs Zapier/Make bridge, and what must NOT be duplicated in GHL.

**Compliance surface** - health, finance, legal, insurance, debt, cannabis.
Drives: AI guardrails, SMS content rules, consent copy, A2P registration difficulty.

**Channels the customer actually uses** - SMS, email, WhatsApp, IG DM, FB Messenger, GBP chat,
phone. Drives: Conversation AI channel enablement and which inboxes need staffing.

**Reactivation asset** - is there a dormant customer list? How big? Drives whether the first
30-day win is a database reactivation campaign (it usually is).

**Who maintains this after handoff** - agency-managed vs client staff. Drives how much is
automated vs left as a Manual Action task, and how heavy the naming discipline must be.
