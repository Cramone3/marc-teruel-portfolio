About me

I'm Marc, a GoHighLevel specialist who builds complete marketing and sales automation systems for small and mid-sized businesses across different industries. I fix the problems that cost businesses leads and revenue - scattered tools, missed follow-ups, and zero visibility into the pipeline by building calendars, workflows, and CRMs that run themselves. I handle the heavy lifting of setup, automation, and integrations so you can focus on running your business instead of babysitting software.

Capabilities & expertise

I build the full GoHighLevel stack from the ground up: calendars, contacts and smart lists, opportunity pipelines, and payment setups tailored to how you actually sell. I configure AI Agents for Voice AI and Conversation AI, and I design end-to-end automation workflows across every trigger and action type: contacts, appointments, opportunities, payments, and communication.

On the acquisition side, I build funnels, websites, forms, chat widgets, and quizzes inside Sites to capture leads straight into your CRM. I set up email infrastructure through LC or third-party SMTP, register A2P 10DLC for compliant SMS, and configure custom fields, tags, custom values, domains, and integrations to tie your entire tech stack together.
