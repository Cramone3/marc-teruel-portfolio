Open Roles

Find Your Place on the Team

Whatever your background, there's a seat for you here.

Basketball Coach / Trainer

Lead drills and skill-building sessions. Share what you know about the game with kids eager to learn.

Youth Mentor

Build real one-on-one relationships with participants — guidance, encouragement, and accountability.

Event Coordinator

Help plan and run camp days and community events — logistics, setup, and day-of coordination.

Media & Storytelling

Capture photos, video, and stories that show our impact and help spread the mission.

Academic Tutor

Support youth with schoolwork, study habits, and college prep. Education is the other half of what we do.

Administrative Support

Help with office tasks, outreach, and donor communications. Remote or on-site options available.
